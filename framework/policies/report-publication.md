# Report Publication Policy

Artifacts have three visibility levels:

- **Private:** raw judge reports, internal disagreement, security details, audit notes, and adjudication deliberation.
- **Team-facing:** constructive evidence-backed feedback about that team, scores authorized by the event, and its matchup history.
- **Public:** concise ceremony-safe results, demonstrated strengths, matchup winner, and non-sensitive rationale.

Do not publish hidden reasoning, another team's private details, secrets, personal data, unsupported allegations, detailed exploitation instructions, or insulting persona language. Public summaries must not introduce claims absent from audited private records. Every published artifact records its visibility level and approval status.
