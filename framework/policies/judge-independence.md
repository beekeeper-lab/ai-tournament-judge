# Judge Independence Policy

Initial judges receive the same pinned evidence manifest, submission rubric, evidence policy, and output template. They may receive their own persona definition. They must not receive another judge's score, notes, report, draft, or conversation.

Run each judge in a fresh context. Save reports only after the judge completes. Corrections for malformed output return to the originating judge with formatting defects, not other judges' conclusions.

The consolidator may inspect all completed reports. Matchup passes remain independent of one another. An audit may inspect all artifacts but may not rewrite substantive judgment merely to increase agreement.
