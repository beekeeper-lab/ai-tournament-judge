# Disagreement and Adjudication Policy

Disagreement is information and must not be hidden by averaging.

Adjudication is required for an `NE` that prevents scoring, a severe criterion disagreement, contradictory factual claims that affect scoring, conflicting order-balanced matchup winners, or a close-call matchup.

The adjudicator receives the disputed claims and original evidence, not an invitation to rescore the entire project. It must state the question, evidence reviewed, factual resolution if possible, impact on the result, confidence, and human decision when uncertainty remains.

Original reports are immutable. Add adjudication as a versioned attachment. A human event official owns disqualification, rules exceptions, and unresolved final ties.
